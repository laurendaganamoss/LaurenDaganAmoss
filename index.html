About

Dr. Lauren Dagan Amoss is an academic researcher, lecturer, and strategic consultant specializing in India’s foreign and security policy, India–Israel relations, and Indo-Pacific strategy. Her work examines India’s rise as a global power, strategic autonomy, security partnerships, deterrence, maritime security, and the implications of Asian geopolitics for Israel and the Middle East.

She serves as an Indo-Pacific Advisor at Israel’s Ministry of Defence and as an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Political Science at Bar-Ilan University. She is also a Senior Research Fellow at the Centre for New Age Warfare Studies (CNAWS), India, and a Research Fellow at the Begin-Sadat Centre for Strategic Studies (BESA), Israel.

Her academic and policy work bridges research, teaching, public engagement, and strategic analysis for academic audiences, security professionals, decision-makers, and the wider public.

Research Interests

India’s Foreign and Security Policy

India’s strategic autonomy, multi-alignment, great-power relations, national security, and the evolution of Indian foreign policy under changing regional and global conditions.

India–Israel Relations

The diplomatic, strategic, technological, and security dimensions of India–Israel relations, from historical distance to contemporary partnership.

Indo-Pacific Strategy

Strategic competition, maritime security, regional partnerships, and the changing balance of power across the Indo-Pacific.

India and the Middle East

India’s growing engagement with Israel, the Gulf, Iran, Egypt, and emerging connectivity initiatives linking Asia, the Middle East, and Europe.

Security, Deterrence, and Counter-Terrorism

India–Pakistan relations, terrorism, deterrence, escalation dynamics, and the strategic implications of conflict under the nuclear shadow.

Global South and International Order

India’s role in the Global South, India–China competition, institutional reform, and competing visions of global governance.

Current Projects

  • India’s Strategic Autonomy under Stress — Research on India’s strategic autonomy, regional security pressures, and the implications of India–China–Pakistan dynamics for India’s global positioning.
  • India–Israel Relations and Strategic Transformation — Analysis of the political, security, technological, and regional dimensions of India–Israel relations.
  • Maritime Security and the Indo-Pacific — Research on India’s maritime partnerships, blue economy, strategic connectivity, and regional security in the Indian Ocean and Indo-Pacific.
